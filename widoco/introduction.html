Introduction back to ToC

An ontology defining the classes and properties commonly required for describing tarot decks and their cards. This includes the suits and ranks that nearly every tarot system has, as well as the extension points that external vocabularies fill, such as for symbology or correspondences.

As well as the ontology itself, this project also includes two example vocabularies of common correspondences and symbology found within the tarot - both of these vocabularies were heavily inspired by the Golden Dawn tradition and the Rider-Waite-Smith deck. We have also provided a series of example decks which demonstrate the ontology being applied in practice to several popular decks. Links to these accompanying resources can be found in the "See Also" section at the top of this page.

Interpretations back to ToC

All data within this project, including the ontology itself, is deliberately designed to avoid prescribing or asserting any definitive interpretation of any particular tarot card. Interpretation is an inherently contextual and deeply personal aspect of tarot reading. We do not believe it is the role of this project, or of any single authority, to dictate how others should interpret the cards or their meanings, and we are confident that this is the prevailing view within the tarot community. Interpretations may vary considerably, and this diversity is something we seek to respect.

That said, this ontology does provide a small number of interpretation-related properties for those who wish to express and document interpretations:

  • tarot:interpretation is a property that can be used to record an individual's or group's own interpretation of a card. We strongly recommend that interpretations are accompanied by provenance information identifying whose interpretation is being represented.
  • tarot:popularInterpretation can be used to describe an interpretation that is widely recognised or accepted within a particular tradition or school of tarot.

Ultimately, the human element is what makes tarot special. This project makes no attempt to establish a definitive or universally authoritative interpretation of any card, nor does it seek to imply that such an interpretation exists. Its purpose is to provide a framework through which interpretations can be represented, attributed, and explored, while leaving the act of interpretation where it belongs: with the individual reader.

Vocabulary reuse back to ToC

The reuse of established vocabularies for generic concepts is encouraged. Not every concept needs to be defined within a tarot-specific ontology, particularly where suitable terms already exist in well-established vocabularies. Reusing existing vocabularies helps avoid unnecessary duplication, promotes interoperability, and makes the project's data easier to integrate with other datasets and knowledge graphs.

Throughout this project, we make use of established vocabularies where appropriate. For example, skos is used to provide labels for concepts, while foaf:depiction is used to link cards to images of them. We encourage others working with this ontology to follow the same approach, reusing existing terms wherever they adequately describe the concepts being represented.
